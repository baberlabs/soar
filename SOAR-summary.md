# SOAR Summary

## What SOAR Is

SOAR is a decentralised, community-owned digital platform designed to restore control, purpose, and agency to time spent online.

It operates as a **community benefit society**, where users become members through share ownership and participate in governance through a one-member-one-vote system. The platform is not owned by a private entity extracting value from users; it is collectively owned and shaped by the people who use it.

SOAR combines a **learning environment, creative workspace, reflective system, and civic platform** into one integrated experience. It is built as a direct alternative to algorithm-driven social media.

At its core, SOAR is not a place to consume content. It is a place to:

- learn deliberately,
- create tangible work,
- reflect on direction,
- connect meaningfully,
- and participate in shaping the system itself.

It is both a **product and a model for a different kind of digital society**.

---

## The Problem SOAR Solves

Modern digital platforms are optimised for attention, not outcomes.

They are built on:

- infinite scrolling,
- algorithmic ranking,
- passive consumption,
- behavioural addiction loops,
- and data extraction models.

These systems lead to:

- reduced attention spans,
- cognitive overload,
- loss of agency,
- shallow engagement,
- and increasing distrust in how data is handled.

Users do not own their activity, their output, or their identity within these systems. Their time produces little lasting value.

SOAR exists to address this directly.

---

## What SOAR Does Differently

SOAR replaces passive consumption with structured, intentional use.

It is built around five core shifts:

- **From scrolling → to completion**
- **From consumption → to creation**
- **From users → to members**
- **From central control → to shared governance**
- **From data extraction → to data ownership**

The platform is designed so that every interaction results in something:

- learned,
- created,
- clarified,
- or contributed.

The goal is not engagement. The goal is progress.

---

## Who SOAR Serves

SOAR is built for individuals seeking intentional, self-directed digital experiences.

Primary audience:

- Gen Z and Millennials
- Creatively and intellectually curious individuals
- People interested in self-development and skill-building
- Users dissatisfied with traditional social media platforms
- Individuals who value ownership, privacy, and meaningful participation

These users are defined by intent rather than demographic category. They want to think, build, and grow — not drift.

## Product Structure

SOAR follows a structured and deliberate user journey:

1. Discover SOAR (website)
2. Understand the model (ownership, no-scroll, purpose)
3. Create account
4. Become a member (purchase £1+ share)
5. Initialise decentralised node (data ownership layer)
6. Complete onboarding (interests + learning preferences)
7. Build Personal Curriculum
8. Enter sessions (learn, create, reflect)
9. Connect with members
10. Participate in governance

This flow sets expectation: SOAR is not passive. It requires participation.

---

## Website vs Platform

SOAR operates across two distinct layers:

### Website (Public Layer)

- Explains the concept and model
- Communicates mission, values, and structure
- Acts as onboarding and entry point
- Enables membership registration

### Platform (Member Layer)

- Full product experience
- Learning, creation, reflection, connection, governance
- Personal data environment
- Decentralised node interaction

This separation reinforces clarity: understanding comes before participation.

---

## Core Principles

### No Scroll

There is no infinite feed.

SOAR removes:

- endless timelines
- algorithmic recommendations
- passive browsing loops

All interactions are **destination-based**.

Users move between:

- learning sessions,
- creation spaces,
- reflection tools,
- governance areas.

The system is designed for completion, not continuation.

---

### Data Ownership

Members own:

- their learning history,
- their creations,
- their reflections,
- their identity and activity.

Data is treated as **personal property**, not platform residue.

---

### Governance

SOAR operates democratically.

- One member = one share = one vote
- Members propose ideas
- Members vote on decisions
- Outcomes are visible and implemented

Future governance includes:

- structured proposal systems
- quarterly member meetings
- potential member councils

Governance is not symbolic. It is functional.

---

### Decentralised Infrastructure

SOAR is built on a decentralised architecture.

Key characteristics:

- Node-based system (user-controlled environments)
- Distributed storage (e.g. IPFS-style systems)
- Content-addressed data
- No single point of control or failure

This ensures:

- data portability,
- resilience,
- transparency,
- user autonomy.

---

### Tech for Good

SOAR positions itself within the **Tech for Good** movement.

It prioritises:

- ethical design,
- user wellbeing,
- transparency,
- community value over profit extraction.

## Product Experience

### Personal Curriculum (Core System)

The central feature of SOAR.

Users:

- choose subjects,
- define timeframes,
- follow structured paths,
- complete learning cycles.

Learning is:

- self-directed,
- finite,
- outcome-based.

This is not a content library. It is a **user-owned curriculum system**.

---

### Session Experience

Each lesson becomes a focused session.

A session includes:

- clear objective
- key information upfront
- prompts and questions
- structured progression
- optional visuals

It is designed as an **active working environment**, not passive reading.

---

### Create

Users produce outputs linked to their learning:

- writing,
- visuals,
- recordings,
- projects.

Creation provides proof of progress and builds a personal body of work.

---

### Reflect (Monthly System)

Reflection operates on a recurring monthly cycle.

Includes:

- vision/mood boards
- intention setting
- letters to future self

This introduces a **time-based rhythm**, not just static features.

It connects past, present, and future behaviour.

---

### Connect

Connection is:

- interest-based
- intentional
- non-algorithmic

Features:

- matching based on shared interests
- direct communication
- collaboration potential

Also includes:

- local events
- workshops
- real-world community interaction

---

### Forum (Civic Layer)

Structured governance environment.

Members can:

- propose platform changes
- discuss improvements
- vote on outcomes

This is the operational core of shared ownership.

---

### Account

A combined identity and control space:

- profile
- activity
- progress
- membership status
- node/data control
