**MANIFESTO**

SOAR believes that users of digital platforms should be in control of their data. We are a pioneer in collective-ownership and open-source decentralised networks, imagining a world where users of technology have digital autonomy to control their online identity.

We believe that technology should be a force for good and utilised for nutritional purposes. We refuse to let technology ruin our focus and attention by providing users with the opportunity to expand knowledge through our personal curriculum lessons, challenging yourselves and your brains and as a result watch your potential SOAR.

Join to become a member of our community and become a part of something special with SOAR.

**WHAT IS SOAR ABOUT**

SOAR is more than just a digital platform, it’s a community of people challenging ‘big tech’ and harmful social platform, fighting as part of a wider societal movement towards community-owned infrastructures and data sovereignty, where users feel safe and free in digital spaces.

**MISSION**

To create a digital platform that challenges the current market. Rather than encouraging mindless scrolling and easily accessible solutions, SOAR empowers users to take control into their own hands, helping to unlock their potential while engaging in mentally stimulating activities.

**VISION**

SOAR envisions a world where digital technology and platforms prioritise collective ownership over private, encouraging individuals to take back agency and drive change in the digital sphere of social networking platforms for the future.

**HOW DID THE IDEA FOR SOAR COME ABOUT?**

- **Challenging Passivity:** To combat the detrimental effects of ‘brain rot’ and the accelerated use of AI for everyday use. SOAR is on a mission to constantly challenge consumers to engage in activities as to not lapse into passivity, encouraging users to exercise autonomy while utilising digital platforms.

- **Creative Outlet:** SOAR wants to offer a space where users have trust and therefore comfortable to creatively engage their brain, in turn achieving freedom of expression.

- **Create Community:** Community is as important for ever as society navigates an unpredictable social, political and economic environment. Through its collective ownership, SOAR is aiming to provides users with a community that is working together towards important common goals to have a positive impact on society.

- **Big Tech Distrust:** According to ONS around 1 in 2 adults reported little or no trust in “Big Tech” companies and more than half of them strongly stating that they would like to have more control over their identity online. SOAR is providing a space away from these platforms where users have no control of what happens to their data to give them the power back.

**KEY PRINCIPLE: Radical and Inclusive Curriculum (use information below)**

Upon creating SOAR, it was important that we were more than just a learning platform. SOAR values run deeper than expanding knowledge, which is where radical and inclusive curriculum design comes into it.

This model addresses topics currently ignored by current theories of education/curriculum, that are arguably deeply linked to the human condition and are fundamental to human education as they are universal and timeless like meditation. Which is essential to achieving self-knowledge and, therefore, a deep form of self-education aligned with the radical and inclusive approach.

SOAR aim has always been to provide their users with a sense of freedom and educational liberation. By aligning with this radical alternative SOAR is fostering an educational shift that propels users towards deep self-knowledge and toward freedom and full awareness of the self, which is reportedly the most important contrast of all.

This curriculum is designed to create a more complete education that embraces full consciousness, wisdom, and mastery in the area of personal and professional competencies. Concepts to achieve this include “life-long” and “ongoing” education that SOAR provides through our personal curriculum which constantly challenges users ot be learning not just for the benefit of hitting a milestone but to constantly engage their brain to unlock a higher version of themselves.

SOAR’s approach to education aligns with this radical approach as they believe “Education and curricula should pay attention to what is most important to human beings, whether they consciously require this or not, and not to what is generally given the most weight by society” .

The approach argues the wing of our inner life, that of de conditioning, self-knowledge, and the awakening of consciousness, is absent from our education, andin turn SOAR brings this into their educational set up. Through championing self-knowledge, deconditioning individuals from big tech algorithmic control and awakening of consciousness from ‘brain rotting social medias platforms.
